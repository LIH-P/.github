
# Laboratorio de Interacciones Hospedero-Patógeno 

Research on host–pathogen interactions, molecular parasitology, and the genomics of trypanosomatids.

## Research software and resources

- [Genome comparison and visualization tools](https://github.com/Gon1976/TcruziGenome) — Scripts for comparative genomics and genome visualization.
- [Trans-Sialidases Curation](https://github.com/Gon1976/Trans-Sialidases-curation) — Manual curation of *Trypanosoma cruzi* trans-sialidase genes.
